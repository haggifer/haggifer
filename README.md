## Projects

### AdLens: AI policy checks for video ads

**[Live](https://www.useadlens.com) · [Example reports](https://www.useadlens.com/demo)**

Upload a video ad and say where it will run. AdLens checks it against several hundred platform ad-policy rules with
Gemini, and each finding is timestamped, cites its policy, and jumps the video to the moment when clicked.

### Likely Weather: ten weather models side by side

**[Live](https://likelyweather.com) · [Android app](https://likelyweather.com/app/likely-weather.apk)** (APK, Android 7.0+)

Compare what ten weather models forecast for any city, and see where they disagree. Viewers can hide or highlight
any model, switch between eight variables, and turn any chart into a table. An archive shows the past weather of any
city hour by hour, for any period of up to 12 days back to 1940.

| Repo                                                       | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [likelyweather](https://github.com/haggifer/likelyweather) | pnpm and Turborepo monorepo: Next.js web app, Expo (React Native) Android app with a home-screen widget, Express API on Postgres and Redis, AWS Lambda and CloudFront via CDK; charts in D3 on the web and Skia in the app |
